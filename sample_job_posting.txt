Senior Project Manager - Multifamily Construction

Company: ABC Construction

We are seeking an experienced Senior Project Manager to lead multifamily construction projects in the Seattle area. This role will oversee projects ranging from $15M to $50M in value.

Requirements:
• 10+ years of construction project management experience
• 5+ years managing multifamily or mixed-use projects
• Proven track record with podium-style construction (Type III/V)
• Strong experience with wood frame and concrete construction
• Expertise in schedule management and budget control
• Proficiency with Procore and Primavera P6
• Excellent subcontractor management skills
• Strong client relations and communication abilities
• Experience with Lean Construction methodologies (Last Planner System preferred)
• LEED AP or PMP certification a plus
• Bachelor's degree in Construction Management, Civil Engineering, or related field

Responsibilities:
• Manage all aspects of multifamily construction projects from preconstruction through closeout
• Develop and maintain detailed project schedules using Primavera P6
• Control project budgets, forecast costs, and manage change orders
• Coordinate with architects, engineers, and subcontractors
• Ensure compliance with safety standards (OSHA) and quality requirements
• Lead project team meetings and implement Last Planner System
• Build and maintain strong relationships with clients and stakeholders
• Manage RFIs, submittals, and project documentation
• Conduct regular site visits and inspections
• Mentor junior project managers and field staff

Projects typically include:
• 4-6 story podium-style apartment buildings
• 50-200 unit multifamily developments
• Mixed-use projects with ground-floor retail
• New construction and renovation work
• LEED-certified sustainable buildings

About Us:
ABC Construction is a leading Seattle-based general contractor specializing in multifamily and mixed-use development. We complete 15-20 projects annually ranging from $10M to $100M.

• Competitive salary ($120K-$150K based on experience)
• Comprehensive health, dental, vision insurance
• 401(k) with company match
• Professional development and training
• Company vehicle or vehicle allowance
